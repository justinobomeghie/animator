This extension adds a single tulip to the Animator as a timed sequence of cubic Bézier paths. A small seed appears first, followed by one curved sage-green stem growing upward. Two long, narrow leaves extend from opposite points along the stem; each outline is followed by a central vein and only a few fine side veins. A small closed bud forms at the tip. The bloom then reveals a taller rear petal and two front petals that curve inward to form the tulip's characteristic cup. Fine pink folds finish the petal layering. The illustration uses rose, ballet-pink, blush, and sage line colors, with no static image or separate animation layer.

The flower uses the existing editor, evaluator, canvas, and animation clock. Each path has its own start and finish times, so the renderer progressively traces the same final design from the seed upward. The completed tulip remains visible for two seconds before the clock restarts the growth sequence. The speed control changes the growth rate and can reverse the sequence; play, pause, reset, and the shape slider continue to work through the existing controls. The protected Point, Line, and Path records and the test directory remain unchanged.
